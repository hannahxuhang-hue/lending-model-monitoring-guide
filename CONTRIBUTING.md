# Contributing and feedback

This guide is written for practitioners at CDFIs, community development credit unions, community banks, and loan
funds. Feedback from people doing this work is what improves it.

## Ways to help

- **Tell me how you used it.** Open a [feedback issue](https://github.com/hannahxuhang-hue/lending-model-monitoring-guide/issues/new?template=feedback.yml): what you tried
  (for example: adopted the checklist or thresholds, ran the spreadsheet on your own model, adapted a template), what worked, and what didn't fit your institution.
- **Point out something unclear or wrong.** Open an issue, quoting the chapter and passage.
- **Suggest a change.** Pull requests are welcome for corrections, clearer wording, or examples. For larger changes,
  open an issue first so we can discuss the approach.
- **Prefer email?** Contact me through my GitHub profile. With your permission, I will record the feedback as an
  issue (with your institution named or anonymized, as you prefer) so the revision history stays transparent.

## What not to share

Please don't post customer data, loan-level data, internal thresholds, or anything your institution considers
confidential. Describe your situation in general terms, or use the synthetic data in `examples/`.

## How feedback is used

Changes made in response to feedback are recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and linked to the issue that
prompted them.
